# Submission Checklist (IEEE Transactions)

## A) Manuscript Completeness

- [ ] Title is final and journal-aligned.
- [ ] All author names, affiliations, and corresponding author are finalized in main.tex.
- [ ] Abstract and keywords respect journal scope and style.
- [ ] Contributions are explicit in Introduction and novelty statement.
- [ ] Related work is complete and up to date.
- [ ] Experimental setup and reproducibility details are explicit.
- [ ] Conclusion states limitations and future work.

## B) Mandatory Editorial Statements

- [ ] Funding statement finalized.
- [ ] Conflict of interest statement finalized.
- [ ] Data availability statement finalized.
- [ ] Code availability statement finalized.
- [ ] Author contributions finalized.
- [ ] Acknowledgment section finalized.

## C) Bibliography and Citations

- [ ] BibTeX entries are complete (authors, venue, year, DOI where available).
- [ ] No placeholder references remain.
- [ ] In-text citations are consistent with IEEE style.

## D) Figures and Tables

- [ ] All figures are publication-quality (vector preferred).
- [ ] Captions are self-contained.
- [ ] Tables are readable and referenced in text.
- [ ] All cross-references compile correctly.

## E) IEEE Compliance

- [ ] Manuscript uses IEEEtran and journal formatting constraints.
- [ ] PDF passes IEEE PDF checker (if required by journal).
- [ ] Page limits and supplementary material policy verified.
- [ ] Cover letter prepared and customized.

## F) Final Packaging

- [ ] main.tex compiles cleanly (no blocking errors).
- [ ] references.bib compiles and resolves all citations.
- [ ] Final PDF generated and reviewed by all authors.
- [ ] Source package archived for resubmission/revision.
