\section{Protocol Message Summary}
Table~\ref{tab:proto} summarizes the main southbound messages used during enrollment and liveness supervision.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Southbound protocol messages (simplified).}
\label{tab:proto}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Message} & \textbf{Direction} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
EnrollmentRequest & Device $\rightarrow$ Gateway & Start enrollment \\
EnrollmentAccepted & Gateway $\rightarrow$ Device & Accept handshake \\
PublicKey & Device $\rightarrow$ Gateway & Identity association \\
EnrollmentCompleted & Gateway $\rightarrow$ Device & Finalize enrollment \\
Heartbeat & Device $\rightarrow$ Gateway & Liveness signal \\
HeartbeatAck & Gateway $\rightarrow$ Device & Liveness acknowledgment \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The simplified summary is sufficient to clarify the paper's control semantics. A production-ready protocol specification should additionally document framing conventions, error codes, retry behavior, and field-level schema constraints.
