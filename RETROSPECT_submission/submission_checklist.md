# Submission Checklist (Computer Communications VSI)

## A) Manuscript Completeness

- [ ] Title is final and aligned with the special issue scope.
- [ ] All author names, affiliations, emails, and corresponding author are finalized in `main.tex`.
- [ ] Abstract and keywords emphasize sustainable digital research infrastructures and edge-to-cloud orchestration.
- [ ] Highlights are present and concise.
- [ ] Contributions and novelty are explicit in the Introduction.
- [ ] Related work includes SLICES and reproducible testbed positioning.
- [ ] Experimental setup and reproducibility details are explicit.
- [ ] Limitations distinguish orchestration validation from direct energy/carbon measurements.

## B) Mandatory Editorial Statements

- [ ] Funding statement finalized.
- [ ] Conflict of interest statement finalized.
- [ ] Data availability statement finalized.
- [ ] Code availability statement finalized.
- [ ] Author contributions finalized.

## C) Bibliography and Citations

- [ ] BibTeX entries are complete, including DOI where available.
- [ ] No placeholder references remain.
- [ ] In-text citations compile with CAS/natbib numeric style.

## D) Figures and Tables

- [ ] Figures are publication-quality and all source files are included.
- [ ] Captions are self-contained.
- [ ] Tables are readable in two-column CAS format.
- [ ] All cross-references compile correctly.

## E) Elsevier CAS Compliance

- [ ] Manuscript uses `cas-dc` from `els-cas-templates`.
- [ ] Bibliography uses `els-cas-templates/cas-model2-names`.
- [ ] Article type selected as `VSI: Sustainable Digital Research Infrastructures` in Editorial Manager.
- [ ] Guide for Authors checked for graphical abstract, highlights, and supplementary material rules.

## F) Final Packaging

- [ ] `main.tex` compiles without blocking errors.
- [ ] `references.bib` resolves all citations.
- [ ] Final PDF reviewed by all authors.
- [ ] Source package includes `main.tex`, `sections/`, `appendices/`, `figures/`, `references.bib`, and `els-cas-templates/`.
